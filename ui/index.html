chonkytasks
Record Process Loops Automate

Show it once. We find the chonky tasks.

Record yourself doing a task, or drop in screenshots. chonkytasks spots the steps you repeat, measures the time they eat, and turns the biggest one into an automation you can run.

Record your screen Press start, go do the task, then come back and press stop. Talk as you go: say why, not what.
